# Account & Data Deletion

Ecolink users can request deletion of their account and associated data at any time — whether or not the app is still installed.

## How to request deletion

Send an email to **tahltech0@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Account Deletion Request"**, from the email address registered on your Ecolink account. Include your full name and registered region so we can locate your account.

## What gets deleted

Once we verify your request, within **7 days** we will permanently delete:

- Your account profile (full name, email, phone number, region, district)
- Your saved bookmarks
- Your push notification token
- Your login credentials

## What may be retained

- **Flood reports you submitted** may be retained in anonymized form (with your name and account link removed) so they continue to serve the community as public safety records.
- We may retain minimal technical logs (e.g. request logs) for a limited period as required for security and fraud-prevention purposes, in line with our [Privacy Policy](./index.md).

## Timeframe

We aim to complete all deletion requests within **7 days** of verification. You'll receive a confirmation email once your data has been deleted.

## Questions

Contact us at **tahltech0@gmail.com** for any questions about this process.
