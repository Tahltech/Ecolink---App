# Privacy Policy for Ecolink

**Last updated:** August 14, 2026

Ecolink ("the App", "we", "us") is a climate-awareness mobile application developed by Tahltech, providing weather forecasts, climate tips, flood reporting, and environmental news for users in Cameroon. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account information
When you register, we collect your **full name, email address, phone number, region, and district**. Your password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and is never stored by us in plain text.

### Location data
With your permission, the App accesses your device's **GPS location** to determine your region and show you locally relevant weather, climate, and flood information. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings; the App will still work using a manually selected or previously saved region. If you are signed in, your resolved region may be saved to your account profile.

### Photos
If you choose to attach a photo to a flood report, the App accesses your **photo library** (with your permission) to select and upload that image. We only access photos you explicitly choose to share.

### Flood reports
Any flood report you submit — including description, location/region, and optional photo — is stored so it can be shown to other users and, where applicable, verified by moderators.

### Push notification token
If you enable notifications, we generate a **push notification token** (via the Expo push service) tied to your device, so we can send you climate tips, weather alerts, and flood alerts. We do not use this token for anything else.

### Saved articles / bookmarks
Articles you bookmark are linked to your account so you can find them again later.

### Automatically collected information
Our backend logs standard technical data (such as request timestamps and error logs) for security and debugging purposes. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

- Create and manage your account
- Show weather, forecast, and climate data relevant to your region
- Let you submit and view community flood reports
- Send you climate tips, weather alerts, and flood-related notifications (if enabled)
- Save your bookmarked articles
- Maintain the security and reliability of the App

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Third-Party Services

To provide the App's features, limited data is shared with the following service providers, only as needed for them to perform their function:

- **Supabase** — hosts our database and handles authentication
- **Render** — hosts our backend server
- **Expo** — delivers push notifications to your device
- **OpenWeatherMap / Open-Meteo** — provides weather and forecast data (only your coordinates/region are sent, never your name or contact details)
- **OpenAQ** — provides air quality data (region-based, no personal data)
- **NewsAPI** — provides climate-related news articles

These providers only receive the minimum information needed to deliver their service and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase's infrastructure and protected using industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the App and our servers and row-level security on our database. While we work to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

- Deny or revoke location and photo-library permissions at any time via your device settings
- Disable push notifications at any time via your device settings
- Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data by contacting us (see below)

To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us at the address below with the subject "Account Deletion Request."

## 6. Children's Privacy

Ecolink is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
